## 更新内容

- **快捷搜索启动面板**。默认按 `Alt+Space` 打开，可搜索已安装应用和指定文件夹中的文件，也可输入网址。支持方向键选择、回车打开，并可在设置里更改快捷键和搜索目录。
- **修复双屏截图时笔记本内置屏整块黑**。受影响的内置屏报告已启用高级色彩，但 DXGI 报告 SDR 色彩空间。旧版把该屏交给 HDR 桌面复制，收到全零帧后又把该屏记为已覆盖。现在结合 DXGI 色彩空间选择抓屏路径，整屏抓取中失败或全黑的 HDR 输出会单独用 GDI `BitBlt` 补抓。

### 验证范围

- 在原来的 125% / 150% 双屏、顶部错开 41 个物理像素的机器上，验证了两屏桌面暗化和内置屏框选。临时启用外接屏 HDR10 后，也验证了 HDR10 与 SDR 混合抓取及覆盖层。
- 快捷启动的浏览器回归覆盖过滤、排序、键盘选择和打开回调。真实桌面上的全局快捷键、失焦关闭、跨屏弹出仍未亲验。真实桌面上打开应用、文件和网址也待验证。
- 截图仍待实机验证的场景包括 scRGB、纯 SDR 单屏、纯 HDR 单屏、跨屏区域抓取、长截图与贴图编辑的完整流程，以及退出截图时显示器重排。跨屏 HDR 区域抓取目前只取一个相交的 HDR 输出。

## What's new

- **Quick launcher**. Press `Alt+Space` by default to search installed apps and files in selected folders, or enter a URL. Use the arrow keys to select an entry and Enter to open it. The hotkey and search folders are configurable in Settings.
- **Fixed a black laptop display during dual-monitor screenshots**. The affected display reported Advanced Color as enabled while DXGI reported an SDR color space. Earlier versions sent that display through HDR desktop duplication, received an all-zero frame, and marked the display as covered. Capture now checks the DXGI color space. During full-desktop capture, a failed or all-black HDR output is recaptured individually with GDI `BitBlt`.

### Validation scope

- On the original dual-monitor setup at 125% and 150% scaling with a 41-physical-pixel vertical offset, both displays dimmed and the selection rendered on the laptop display. Mixed HDR10 and SDR capture and overlay rendering were also checked after temporarily enabling HDR10 on the external display.
- Launcher browser regression tests cover filtering, ranking, keyboard selection, and open callbacks. The global hotkey, dismissal on focus loss, and opening the panel on either monitor have yet to be verified on a real desktop. Opening apps, files, and URLs on the real desktop also awaits verification.
- Screenshot scenarios still awaiting hardware verification include scRGB, SDR-only and HDR-only single-monitor setups, cross-monitor region capture, the full scrolling capture and pinned-image editing workflows, and display rearrangement while exiting capture. HDR region capture currently uses one intersecting HDR output.
