## 更新内容

岛上原有的 AI 会话区改为轻量的 **Cindy 通知**。

- **移除 AI 会话区**：不再显示 Agent 页面、会话列表与卡片、运行状态灯、上下文占用及会话预览设置。
- **四类通知**：任务完成、需要你回答、定时与后台任务、出错。折叠岛只显示铃铛与待处理数量，点击尝试把 Cindy 窗口切到前台，不再展开会话面板。
- **统一设置**：「Cindy 通知」页提供总开关、四类事件开关、提示音开关，以及数据库路径、读取时间和失败原因。
- **只读接入**：读取 Cindy 本地数据库，不写入 Cindy 数据。岛上的本地时间水位避免重复提醒；首次启用不补报启动前的事件。旧布局和设置入口兼容迁移，旧钩子的一次性清理保留。

截图能力、悬浮胶囊外形和跟随应用语言的托盘菜单继续保留。

### 兼容性与验收限制

Cindy 的数据库属于内部结构：缺少所需表或字段时，对应类别会降级为空；后台任务从 `subagent_runs` 推断，未提供统一后台标记。完成、错误和定时/后台结果只查看最近 24 小时，等待回答的事件不受此窗口限制，仍需晚于本地已读水位。

已通过前端检查（202 用例）、Rust 测试（200 通过、6 忽略）、clippy、13 页浏览器回归及真实 Cindy 数据库读取。浏览器回归覆盖角标出现、点击调用窗口切换与推进水位；真实桌面的「新通知角标出现 → 点击切到 Cindy」尚未完成验收，不视为已验证。

## What's new

The former AI session area is replaced by lightweight **Cindy notifications**.

- **Remove the AI session area**: the Agent page, session list and cards, activity indicator, context occupancy and session preview settings are gone.
- **Four notification categories**: task completion, questions awaiting your answer, scheduled/background tasks and errors. The collapsed island shows a bell and pending count. Clicking attempts to bring Cindy to the foreground without opening a session panel.
- **One settings page**: Cindy Notifications provides a master switch, four category switches, a sound switch and database diagnostics including path, read time and failure reason.
- **Read-only integration**: Cindy's local database is never modified. A local timestamp watermark avoids repeat alerts; first use does not replay events predating startup. Old layout values and settings links migrate, and one-time legacy hook cleanup remains.

Screenshot capabilities, the floating capsule shape and app-language tray menus are preserved.

### Compatibility and validation limits

Cindy's database is an internal interface. Missing tables or required columns disable the corresponding category. Background work is inferred from `subagent_runs`, which has no unified background flag. Completion, error and scheduled/background results are limited to the last 24 hours. Pending questions have no such age limit but must still be newer than the local seen watermark.

Validation passed: frontend checks (202 tests), Rust tests (200 passed, 6 ignored), clippy, 13 browser regression pages and reads against a real Cindy database. Browser regression covers the badge, focus-command call and watermark advancement. The real-desktop flow of a new badge appearing and clicking it to bring Cindy forward has not completed acceptance and is not claimed as verified.
