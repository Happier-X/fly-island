## 更新内容

灵动岛现在是一颗独立悬浮的胶囊，默认与屏幕顶部留出间距。

- **完整胶囊**：收起时四角对称、左右两端为半圆，基准高度 38 DIP、半径 19 DIP；不再是上方拍平的刘海造型。外观设置中的预览和排列编辑器同步更新。
- **顶部留白**：默认在显示器工作区顶部正中留 10 DIP 间距；回到默认位置、拖回顶部吸附时使用相同间距。已有自定义位置、多显示器选择与闲置贴边隐藏设置继续保留。
- **连续开合**：展开为四角 30 DIP 的圆角面板，宽、高与圆角连续变化，上缘保持稳定；展开 480ms、收起 380ms，去掉展开过冲。快速反复开合从当前形态继续，翻译页也采用连续展开；系统开启减少动态效果时停止过渡。
- **托盘语言**：托盘菜单跟随应用的中英文设置。简体中文显示「隐藏灵动岛／显示灵动岛」「设置」「退出」，英文保留对应英文菜单。

本版不包含仍在单独验收的截图／PixPin 静态能力补充及截图图标改动；截图功能沿用 v3.2.1。

## What's new

The island now floats as a complete capsule, with a small gap below the top of the screen.

- **A full capsule**: the collapsed island has symmetric circular ends, with a base height of 38 DIP and a radius of 19 DIP. The former flat-top notch shape is gone. Appearance previews and the layout editor match the new outline.
- **Space above the island**: the default position is centered 10 DIP below the monitor's work-area top. Resetting the position and snapping near the top use the same gap. Saved custom positions, monitor selection and idle edge hiding are preserved.
- **Continuous opening and closing**: the capsule becomes a panel with 30 DIP circular corners. Width, height and radii transition together while the top stays fixed. Opening takes 480ms without overshoot; closing takes 380ms. Repeated toggles continue from the current shape, including the translation page. Reduced-motion settings disable these transitions.
- **Tray language**: the tray menu follows the app's Chinese or English language setting, including Show Island / Hide Island, Settings and Quit.

This release excludes the screenshot capability and icon changes awaiting separate acceptance. Screenshot behavior remains as in v3.2.1.
